## VesperTV 1.0.41 (beta)

### New
- **Goals alert:** Settings > Interface > Goals alert. Opt in to a compact top-left score popup for teams in My Teams, with team and league logos, above any screen or stream. Game reminders stay top-right. Alerts follow the score feed, respect Hide scores, and do not replay old scores when you open the app.
- **Media Hub stream sources:** press Right during playback to browse alternative files with source, format and file-size details. Switch while keeping your position. The TV drawer is smaller, and Back or Left closes it without leaving playback.
- **Phone picture-in-picture:** on supported Android phones, press Home during fullscreen playback to keep watching in a floating window.
- **Playback settings:** player options now share a Playback category, including background video, audio, frame rate and resolution, Data pool and trailers. Preferred audio/subtitle languages and remembered selections help movies and episodes reopen with the tracks you chose. Audio-language preferences are available on Android.
- **Home:** optionally hide the profiles row under Settings > Interface > Tabs.
- **Sports Trak:** optional Boxing, WWE and AEW entries in the league bank, using the existing event-card layout. Schedule coverage depends on the feed.

### Fixes and improvements
- Media Hub movie/episode resume has stronger file matching when source links change, with clearer file-size information.
- Windows now saves and restores movie and episode progress, including the Resume button on detail pages.
- M3U movies and shows keep on-demand controls instead of being treated as live TV.
- Back from Home returns to TV without also opening the exit prompt.
- Live player information includes a gold tag naming the channel's playlist.
- Saved Media Hub source URLs can be viewed and copied from source settings.
- Sports Trak matching rejects stale event listings and improves team-name matching.

### Install
Android TV, Fire Stick and phones: install the APK over your existing app, use Downloader code **1760256**, or Settings > Check for Updates.
Windows: download the ZIP, extract the whole folder, and run VesperTV.exe.

**Beta:** please use Settings > Send log after a problem. A provider or debrid service being unavailable is separate from app playback. This update does not change the native video player.

## VesperTV 1.0.44 (beta)

### Sports Mode — beta feature
- Make Sports Trak your home from **Settings > Interface > Sports Mode**, or the **Sports Mode** pill in Sports Trak. It is optional and saved separately for each profile.
- Your first five leagues live in a sidebar that opens with Left; the remaining leagues and **Edit leagues** stay in the floating top bar. **Today** opens the day picker. Standings, My Teams, Networks and the other app sections remain available.
- While watching, a bigger video sits beside the day's games: **OK** watches a game, **Right** opens full screen, **hold OK** opens stats, and **Down** past the list returns to the card rows.
- **Sports Mode is a beta feature.** Please report layout or remote-navigation problems through **Settings > Send log**. Turn the switch off to restore the regular home.

### Live TV and programme schedules
- On Android, open **Channel schedule** from the live information card with **OK > Up > OK**. Rewind/Fast-forward page through the schedule.
- Upcoming programmes now offer **Remind me** and scheduled **Record** on Android. Reminders appear while VesperTV is open; recordings require the box to stay powered, provider access and enough storage.
- The Android live information card is smaller, with Channel schedule inside it. Optional group counts show how many channels, movies or shows a group contains.

### Media Hub and search
- Compact source cards group each source's controls together and distinguish playback sources from catalogue sources. Add-source and connector controls stay at the top, with improved remote navigation.
- Broader title matching handles spaces and punctuation, such as **spiderman** and **Spider-Man**, in global and Media Hub searches.
- A **Newest** filter highlights this year's releases. Refresh rechecks sources, search results identify their source, and IMDb source lookup has wider coverage.
- Media Hub subtitle support and preferred-language choices are improved. Movie/episode buffering after seeking has been adjusted.

### More improvements
- Added a warm **Red** palette and replaced Habs with **Blue**. Existing Habs selections automatically use Blue.
- Sports team schedules include a calendar and football cup fixtures, with improved matching for channels named using team codes.
- Android phone player controls fit more comfortably around the navigation bar. Preferred subtitle languages appear first.
- Android catch-up has improved TS fallback and stops at the programme's end.

### Install
Android TV, Fire Stick and phones: install the APK over your existing app, use Downloader code **1760256**, or **Settings > Check for Updates**.

Windows: download the new ZIP, extract the whole folder, and run **VesperTV.exe**. Both packages are **1.0.44, build 492**.

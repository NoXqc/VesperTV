## Shared design and behavior

- Rounded dark glass, a diagonal reflection, a fine rim and a soft shadow;
  one clipped 12px backdrop blur per panel. Accent colors follow the theme.
  Light app themes get readable dark-menu foregrounds/accents too.
- `TvDialogOption`, `TvMenuTile` and roll-down rows share the glass treatment
  within these panels. The focused option keeps a clear 2px accent outline.
  Refined finish enhances the reflection; it does not add another blur per row.
- Existing focusable widgets, action ids, callbacks, disabled states, selected
  markers, Cancel defaults, held-key suppression and Back results are retained.
  No new strings or menu actions were added.
- Standard menu bodies scroll within a bounded panel. Titles wrap to two lines
  before ellipsis; their complete text remains available to semantics. Footer
  actions stay reachable with keyboard insets and large text.
- The selected `TvDialogOption` is revealed after focus/layout. Anchored menus
  open above a low anchor when the space below is insufficient, and stay inside
  safe screen/keyboard bounds.
- Specialised game/reminder menus keep their existing content layout and
  scrolling through `GlassMenuDialog.custom`. Default dialog width cap is 560;
  the network card menu retains its 760 cap. Anchored menus keep their width.

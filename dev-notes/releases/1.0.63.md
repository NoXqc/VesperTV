## VesperTV 1.0.63 (beta)

Android TV, Fire TV and Android phones - build 698. Windows and Linux stay on 1.0.61 for now.

### ✨ Home mini player
- A small player at the top right of **Home** plays the live channel your remote is resting on (a moment after you stop pressing, so scrolling past doesn't start it). **OK** opens it full screen on the same stream.
- **Play/Pause** on the playing card, **Stop**, or hold OK > **Stop mini player** turns it off until you play a card again.
- Settings > Interface > Tabs > **Mini player on Home** (on by default on TV boxes; phones don't have it).
- Home now fills **Recent channels, Continue Watching and Favourite channels** as soon as they've loaded after a cold start.

### ✨ Media Hub: the right stream for your language
- Streams are ranked by your language. A stream that lists several languages ("English | Russian | Ukrainian") now counts as English when English is in the list; a stream with only other languages goes last.
- **Automatically play the largest file** now picks the largest file that has your language, not the largest English-only one (a 112 GB remux with English among other languages no longer loses to a 76 GB English-only one). It also wins over **Auto-play preferred stream** when both are on.
- The audio picker names the language ("English (Original)").
- **Dolby Atmos labels** for MKV files in the Media Hub: TrueHD Atmos and E-AC-3 Atmos now show up instead of "E-AC-3 5.1".

### ✨ Vesper AI
- **Media Hub for AI apps**: search a title, see every stream with its quality, audio, languages and size, play the best one or a chosen one, switch stream at the same position when it buffers, next / previous episode, and switch **audio track** or **subtitles**.
- The streams list now always includes the one it would play and says **how it was picked** (it used to be missing when it sat far down a long list).
- The AI can now change the **Media Hub playback options** (largest file, auto-play, audio language, maximum quality, prefer SDR).
- **Favourites**: ask it to move a channel to the top (or up / down, or to a position) or to set a whole order: "CP24 first, then my Canadian channels, then US, then my 24/7 shows".
- **Your own groups**: "make a Sports group with the sports channels from my favourites". It can create a group, add or remove channels, put them in order, move a group and delete it (the TV asks for OK first). Channels in your group show only there, like Move to group on the TV.
- Tip: if your AI app doesn't see the new abilities, open VesperTV on the TV, reconnect the VesperTV connector in the app and start a new chat (it keeps an old list).

### Remote and settings
- Settings rows are more compact on TV boxes and computers; the **Launch with profiles selector** switch moved to Settings > Profiles.
- **Fast-forward / Rewind** on the remote jump 10 rows in lists and the guide.

Install:
- **Android**: Downloader code **1760256**, or update from Settings > Check for updates.
- **Windows / Linux**: stay on 1.0.61 (download it from the 1.0.61 release).

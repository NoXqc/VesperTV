## VesperTV 1.0.61 (beta)

Android TV, Fire TV and Android phones - build 683. Windows stays at 1.0.45 for now.

### ✨ Your launcher shows VesperTV (Android TV, Fire TV)
- VesperTV adds rows to launchers that show Android TV's channels, such as **Projectivy Launcher**: **Watch Next** (Continue watching, the next episode of a show, New episodes and your Watch list) and a **Favourite channels** row. Settings > Interface > TV home screen, on by default.

### ✨ Vesper AI: settings and history
- Ask your own AI, or an AI app such as Claude through your connector, to change settings: turn skip intro off, hide the Movies tab, set Up Next, subtitle size, language, theme, the guide layout, Sports Trak options and about 50 more. Several can change at once with one OK on the TV.
- Ask it to clear your search and channel history, Continue Watching and saved progress, Media Hub history or the watch list, or to reload the TV guide.
- AI apps still wait for OK on the TV (Ask on the TV first, on by default). The AI settings, your PIN, profiles and playlists can't be changed this way, and Kids profiles can't change settings.

### Live TV guide
- **Event channels** ("NBA 01: Nuggets vs. Pacers @ 7 Nov 04:58 PM ET"): when the provider's guide has nothing for them, the programme now comes from the channel's name. Their names also refresh during the day instead of waiting for a full update, so you no longer see yesterday's games.
- **Preview on highlight** (opt-in, Settings > Interface > Guide): the channel plays after a short moment as you move over it in the list or Timeline guide.
- New **3 hours** choice for the guide's auto-refresh.
- Guide updates are faster, and a guide update that failed tries again later by itself.
- The "Updating..." text in the header no longer stays on after a background update.

### Back and resume
- **Back** on the first screen of the app (the TV tabs, Sports Mode, Who's watching) now leaves straight away on Android, without asking. VesperTV stays ready for an instant return.
- Coming back to the app resumes your last channel again (it stopped after you opened the app from a notification). With the **Who's watching** screen on, picking the same profile resumes their channel; another profile starts fresh.

### Display
- **Match resolution** never goes below 1080p (on one 4K box it dropped to 720p and the menus changed size).

Mac and Windows aren't part of this download.

Install: Downloader code **1760256**, or update from Settings > Check for updates.

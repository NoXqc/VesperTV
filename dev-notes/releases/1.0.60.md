## VesperTV 1.0.60 (beta)

Android TV, Fire TV and Android phones - build 676. Windows stays at 1.0.45 for now.

### ✨ Watch list, by hand
- A **Watch list** button on every movie and show page (your IPTV playlists and Media Hub). Press it again to take the title off.
- **Movies** and **TV Shows** have a **Watch list** row under Continue Watching, and a **Watch list** entry in the groups list to jump to it. Hold OK on a card to remove it.
- **Media Hub > Watch list** shows your Media Hub titles in two rows, **Movies** and **TV Shows**.
- Home's Watch list row shows both kinds. Vesper AI and your AI apps can add Media Hub titles too ("add Dune to my watch list").

### Player
- **Buffer** in the options row under the player bar (live channels): try a bigger live buffer on a channel that keeps stalling without leaving it. Same choices as Settings > Playback > Live buffer.

### Startup
- On the **Updating Content** screen, **Back** now opens the app and the update keeps going behind it (it used to leave the app, and some Fire TVs then stopped the update).
- New **Update in the background** (Settings > General > Startup): when an update is due, open on Live TV right away and update Movies and TV Shows behind it. Off by default.

### Sports Trak
- A **Guide** layout (opt-in): a TV guide-style list of the day's games, with many more on screen.
- **Game card size** (Large / Medium / Small), all Division I college basketball games, and boxing cards and TheSportsDB leagues that were missing games now show up.
- Choosing a different source on a game's menu is remembered when you come back (it could revert to the first one).

### Favourites
- Hold OK on a channel in Favourites (with Mix playlists on) > **Sort favourites**: A-Z, Z-A, or back to your own order.

### Media Hub
- Resuming a movie or episode picks the same file again, matching it by size, and asks you if that file is gone.

### Vesper AI
- Works from your phone when the TV is on a VPN, can set up and undo playlist edits, understands "what channel are the Habs playing tonight", and search ignores "movie" / "show" so IPTV titles match.

### Mac
- A macOS version of VesperTV (thanks to gmtherien), built automatically on GitHub as an unsigned app and a DMG. Not part of this download.

Install: Downloader code **1760256**, or update from Settings > Check for updates.

## VesperTV 1.0.59 (beta)

Android TV, Fire TV and Android phones - build 669. Windows stays at 1.0.45 for now.

### ✨ Vesper AI (new)
Tell VesperTV what you want, by voice or by typing. Open it from the new **Vesper 🎙️** row in the menu, the mic in Search, or your remote's voice button.
- **Built-in commands, free, no AI needed:** "play TSN 1", "mets RDS", "pause", "go back 30 seconds", "next channel", "back to live", "what's on", "what's on TSN 1", "when is the Simpsons on", "open the movies", "search for Dune", "put on the Habs game", "today's games", "add this to my watch list", "help". English, French, Spanish, Italian and German.
- **Talk from your phone:** scan a QR code and type or use your phone keyboard's microphone. Works on Fire TV too, whose remote mic apps can't use.
- **Remote voice button:** Settings > Vesper AI > Remote voice button (off by default). If your remote's button isn't picked up, "Teach it your remote's button" learns it.
- **Bring your own AI** for anything else: Claude, any OpenAI-compatible service (including a local Ollama) or Gemini, with your own key. "Make me a movie night list for Friday", "hide every Turkish group", "what crime shows do I have?". What you ask goes straight from your TV to that AI, never through us.
- **Use it from Claude or ChatGPT:** Settings > Vesper AI > AI apps gives this TV a private connector address. Add it as a custom connector in your AI app and control VesperTV from your phone or computer, by voice or text, with your own subscription. Plays and changes wait for OK on the TV. One connector per TV for now.
- **Watch list:** Vesper and your AI can build a list for later; it shows as a **Watch list** row on Home. Hold OK on a title to remove it.

### Player
- The options row under the player bar is a compact glass pill with round buttons and a status line; the sound info says **Dolby Atmos**.
- Quality badges: 720p is HD, 1080p FHD, 2160p 4K (frame rate has its own badge).
- Opening Multiview from the player hands the playing stream over without reconnecting, and full screen glides in and out of the guide preview.

### Faster start
- The app answers the remote right after the splash: background loading, sync and update checks wait until you've settled in, and Home rows appear one by one.
- **Back > Exit** keeps VesperTV ready for an instant return; the menu's bottom row **Exit VesperTV** closes it fully.

### Big M3U playlists
- Big M3U playlists (movies and series) load in the background into the same catalog as Xtream, with episodes grouped into shows and seasons. Download progress shows in MB, with plain error messages.
- An Xtream "get.php" M3U link is read through its Xtream login when it works.

### Playlist editor
- Use channel logos from your guide (EPG) sources: preview them, pick one, or go back to the provider's logo.

### Sports Trak
- A goal alert follows up when the goal is disallowed after review.

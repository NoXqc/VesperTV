## VesperTV 1.0.64 (beta)

Android TV, Fire TV and Android phones - build 710. Windows stays on 1.0.61 and Linux on 1.0.63 (build 702) for now.

### ✨ Record a show that's on later, or a whole series (Android)
- In the Timeline guide, move to a show that hasn't started and hold OK: **Record this show**. A red dot marks it in the guide; hold OK again to cancel. It is also in the channel menu on the plain list and in **Channel schedule** in the player.
- **Record series…** asks **Every airing** of that show on that channel, or **Skip repeats** (leaves out an episode with the same guide description as one already recorded). Started on a show that is on now, it also starts recording it at once.
- New airings are added as the guide lists them (while the app is open); the ones already scheduled record with the app closed. Up to 8 days ahead and 20 airings per series. An airing that needs a connection your playlist doesn't have is skipped and counted, never forced.
- Recordings > **Series recordings**: one card per series with what is scheduled and the next airing; OK > **Stop series recording** cancels what is scheduled and keeps what is recorded. Cancel one airing and it stays cancelled when the guide updates; if the guide moves a show, the recording follows it.

### ✨ Compact UI
- Settings > Interface > Tabs > **Compact UI**: smaller tabs, groups and channel rows, a slimmer top bar and shorter Timeline rows, so more fit on screen. Each profile keeps its own setting.

### ✨ Downloads and the network folder
- A **Download** button on movie and show pages (IPTV and Media Hub): a movie, an episode or a whole season, for watching offline. It suggests **Tonight at 2:00**, downloads at a speed that looks like someone watching, waits while you watch or record on the account's last connection, and carries on after a restart.
- Recordings and downloads can be saved to a **network folder** (a shared folder on a computer or NAS).
- Recordings > **On the network folder** lists the videos on the share: **copy** one to this device (a tablet before a trip) or play it from the share.
- Download cards take their poster's colours; the footer shows what the network folder holds.

### ✨ Home
- The focused live card shows a big logo with LIVE, what is on now, the channel and times, and the guide's description, with a glow in the logo's colours.
- **Home wallpaper** (Settings > General): a picture per profile, sent from your phone or chosen on a computer. A switch also puts it behind **Who's watching**.
- Mini player: **OK** on a live card plays it, **OK** again opens it full screen, **Play/Pause** turns it off. With Preview on highlight off, scrolling past a card no longer plays it.

### ✨ Player and phones
- **This game**: Left in the full-screen live player starts with the game on that channel and every channel showing it; OK switches and remembers your pick.
- Phones: the touch player now matches the iPhone's (big -10 / play / +10, one seek bar, title with the clock and what is on). In landscape the guide shows 4 rows and the Vesper panel sits above the keyboard.

### ✨ Sports and guide
- **QMJHL / LHJMQ** added; games are matched by city name.
- Event channels that share one guide ID (TSN+ 05-45, ESPN+, Disney+ Events...) each show their own game, and an idle slot says "No game at this time".
- Tennis no longer asks ESPN for a summary it doesn't have, and goal alerts don't wait for one.

### ✨ Media Hub and Vesper
- A metadata add-on (AIOMetadata...) now fills the title page, so the synopsis and episode names follow your language.
- **Vesper**: "continue watching X in Media Hub", "French subtitles", "subtitles off" and "which audio tracks" work in all six languages, without an AI key.

### Fixes
- Back from Home no longer walks on to Who's watching.
- Settings: the last row is no longer hidden under a phone's navigation bar.

Install:
- **Android**: Downloader code **1760256**, or update from Settings > Check for updates.
- **Windows**: stays on 1.0.61 (download it from the 1.0.61 release). **Linux**: 1.0.63, build 702 (from the 1.0.63 release).

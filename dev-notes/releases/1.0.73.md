## VesperTV 1.0.73 (beta)

Android TV, Fire TV, Android phones, **Windows** and **Linux** (beta) - build 963.

### 🗓 Weekly updates + test builds
- From now on there is **one update a week, on Sunday**. Fixes that people are waiting for come out in between as **test builds**.
- New: Settings > Backup & devices > Check for Updates > **Update channel**. Choose *Stable* (the default) or *Test builds*. The choice is for this device only. Test builds get fixes sooner but can have rough edges. Switch back to Stable at any time and you get the next Sunday update.
- Settings > Help shows the full version with its build number, for example "Version 1.0.73 (963)". **Send log** is now in Settings > Help.

### 🔊 Sound and picture (Android)
- **5.1 / Dolby on Fire OS 6 sticks** (Fire TV Stick 4K 1st gen and others): Surround passthrough now also works with Compatibility video, so your receiver gets 5.1 instead of stereo. Thanks to corporate-gadfly ([#12](https://github.com/NoXqc/VesperTV/pull/12)).
- **Big movie files** (40 Mbit/s and up, such as 4K remuxes): when one connection is slower than the file needs, the player opens up to 3 connections. They no longer freeze when the source slows down.
- **DRM channels from M3U playlists** now play: ClearKey keys or a license server (`#KODIPROP`), and stream headers (`#EXTVLCOPT`, `#EXTHTTP`). A channel whose server sends back nothing now says so in plain words.
- **Reload on a 4K channel** no longer ends on an error on boxes with a single 4K decoder.
- **Dispatcharr:** picking another source in the player now takes a few seconds instead of 30-40.

### 📺 Live TV guide
- The bottom channel is no longer cut off.
- Pressing Right past the usual 5 hours opens the next hours, up to 7 days ahead. Back returns to now.
- Left/Right move one programme at a time, so short programmes can be picked.
- Down on the last channel wraps to the first.
- OK on a group goes straight to its first channel, in the list and in the Timeline.
- The channel column has a wider logo box, so wide logos (like Sky's) are no longer tiny. A 4K channel's name on two lines stops before the 4K tag.

### 🎮 Player (Android TV)
- **Move the options row's tiles:** hold OK on a tile, move it with Left/Right, then press OK.
- Settings > Playback > Player: **Player bar transparency** and **Options row transparency**.
- Remote buttons > Left in the player has a new choice, **Back to the guide**.

### 🏟 Sports Trak
- A channel's build-up show before kickoff now counts for the game, and live games keep their channels after the first 30 minutes.
- Show clock also puts the time on Sports Trak's top row.
- **Live now in Sports**, for Android TV launchers such as Projectivy: a launcher row with your leagues' live games and scores. OK on a game plays its channel. Turn it on or off in Settings > Appearance > Tabs > Live games on the TV home screen.

### 🎬 Media Hub and more
- **Silo media server:** Media Hub sources > **Connect Silo**. Sign in with a TV code or a password, then pick a household profile.
- Playlist editor: Duplicates and Test streams are sorted by resolution, then frame rate, then audio (1080/50 above 1080/25, 5.1 above 2.0).
- Title pages: when the buttons wrap onto two lines, the arrows now go through them in order.
- Group management: Right into a tab now lands on a row.
- On TV, buttons look focused only when they really are.
- On weaker boxes, Sports Trak's channel picker and the Settings backgrounds are lighter and smoother.

Install:
- **Android**: Downloader code **1760256**, or update from Settings > Check for updates.
- **Windows**: `VesperTV-windows-x64.zip` from this release (unzip it, then run `VesperTV.exe`), or Settings > Check for updates.
- **Linux** (beta, x64):
  - `VesperTV-linux-x86_64.flatpak` works on any distro with Flathub, the Steam Deck included. Open it in Discover / GNOME Software, or run `flatpak install --user VesperTV-linux-x86_64.flatpak`. It brings its own video library.
  - `VesperTV-linux-x64.tar.gz` is for Ubuntu 24.04+ / Linux Mint 22+. It needs `sudo apt install libmpv2`. Extract it, then run `./vespertv`.

## VesperTV 1.0.69 (beta)

Android TV, Fire TV and Android phones - build 827. **Windows stays on 1.0.68** (no new Windows build); Linux stays on 1.0.63 (build 702).

### ✨ Live player: one bottom bar
- Changing channel now shows **one bar at the bottom** instead of a card at the top plus a bar: clock, logo, channel, playlist, programme, time left, quality / FPS / sound, the progress and the description on two lines. It takes about half the room the old card and bar did. Up and Down keep changing channel.
- Press **OK** and the star, Play / Pause and Channel schedule appear at the right of the description.
- Catch-up, movies, episodes and phones keep their current player bar.

### 🐞 Sync
- **Favourites no longer vanish across synced devices.** A device that had just joined your sync could wipe the favourites on all your devices the first time you starred a channel on it. Update every synced device, then add the favourites back once.
- The Send log now says which settings a sync brings in and how your favourites change.

### ✨ Media Hub
- **Stream badges**: paste a Nuvio / Fusion badge pack address (Settings > Playback > Movies & TV Shows) and sources show its pictures; otherwise VesperTV's own format tags.
- Movies buffer more on boxes with enough memory (less buffering on very large remuxes), and the Send log records why a movie froze.

### ✨ Live TV
- **Sources** in the live player: add or remove other copies of a channel.
- Saved favourites and own groups follow a channel whose id the provider changed.
- Update the guide and playlists **while VesperTV is closed** (Settings > Startup).

### ✨ Multiview
- Multiview shares the box's decoding budget better: a frame saver, 720p copies of big channels and refresh-rate matching.

### ✨ Smaller things
- The seek bar no longer jumps when you press Left / Right on it; held keys grow the step by how long you hold.
- Large-text tab labels show whole words; a **What's New** switch for Movies / TV Shows; Expand catalog rows wrap with Left / Right; the Sports Trak tab has a gold "Trak"; the first Sports card keeps its frame.

Install:
- **Android**: Downloader code **1760256**, or update from Settings > Check for updates.
- **Windows**: 1.0.68 (from the 1.0.68 release).
- **Linux**: 1.0.63, build 702 (from the 1.0.63 release).

## VesperTV 1.0.58 (beta)

Android TV, Fire TV and Android phones - build 632

### New player controls on TV
- **OK** shows the player bar (clock, Play/Pause, favourite) with the channel's info card above it. **Up** goes to the Channel schedule.
- **Down** from the bar opens a row of options: Channels list, Channel schedule, Recall, Watch from the start, Reload channel, Audio track, Subtitles, Audio sync, linked channel, Multiview, Sources, Stream info, Fill screen and Search. **Up** or **Back** returns to the bar.
- With nothing on screen, **Up / Down change channel** on live TV, handy on Fire TV remotes that have no channel buttons.
- A **timeline** in the player bar shows the programme's start, end and how far along it is. On catch-up channels, press Up to select it, then Left to go back in time; back to now returns to live.
- The top bar is gone on TV. Everything hides after 6 seconds without a key. Phones keep their touch controls.

### Audio sync
- New **Audio sync** in the options row and in Settings > Playback > Audio > Audio delay: if the sound is ahead of or behind the picture, press Right (sound later) or Left (sound earlier), up to 5 seconds either way. Hold to go faster. It changes as you press, so you can line it up by eye.
- The **ATMOS** badge shows on Dolby Atmos audio, and audio codecs have readable names.
- Data pool (stream details) is now on by default and opens from **Stream info** in the options row.

### Notifications
- **Notify me** on a TV show page tells you when a new episode is available, with a **New episodes** row on Home and an Android notification, now also while the app is closed.
- **Phone notifications** for Sports Trak (Settings > Sports Trak > Phone notifications): game reminders and goal alerts arrive even when the app is closed. While a followed game is live you'll see a "Following goals" notification with a Stop button.

### Media Hub
- **Live TV**: add-ons that list live TV channels get their own Live TV section in Media Hub, with genre rows for big lists. Channels play like live TV: Up/Down change channel, and Sources lists the other streams. Stremio "tv" add-ons are detected by themselves; for others, turn on **TV add-on** on the source's card.
- **Enabled** and **Show under Other** are now per profile: turning a source off on one profile no longer turns it off for everyone. Removing a source still removes it for every profile, and the app now says so.
- Faster browsing: rows stay anchored as you move, and big cards open after 2 seconds on a title.
- TMDB catalog add-ons are accepted.

### Sync between devices (new)
- Settings > Sync links up to 3 devices with a code, no account needed. Playlists, every profile's favourites, Continue Watching, watch positions, Sports Trak, Media Hub and settings stay in step. Everything is encrypted on your device; only devices with your code can read it.

### Playlist editor (test flight)
- Duplicates follow your renames, you can link a channel to a guide channel or add extra XMLTV guide links, and stream tests mark real 4K channels.

### Sports Trak
- Picks the channel actually showing each game, and games at the same time no longer share one station.
- Peruvian Liga 1 and more soccer competitions; the league bank is grouped by sport.
- Goal alerts wait for the scorer's name.

### Other
- Adjustable guide transparency, Live TV / Sports Trak visibility settings, tidier hold menus.
- Big M3U playlists load in the background.
- Home: Left on a row's first card stays on Home.
- Google TV keyboard fix while typing.

### Update (build 632)
- The guide transparency slider no longer traps the remote: Left/Right change it, Up/Down move on.
- The guide layout (Live or Timeline, full-screen video, transparency, compact, expand) is now per profile.

### Windows
Windows remains at **1.0.45**. This release contains an Android APK only.

### Install

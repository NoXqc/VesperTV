## VesperTV 1.0.62 (beta)

Android TV, Fire TV and Android phones - build 689. Windows and Linux stay on 1.0.61 for now.

### ✨ Wide cards
- New **Wide cards** switch (Settings > Interface > Tabs): the Movies and TV Shows rows and the Expand catalog grid show bigger **16:9 cards** with each title's backdrop, instead of small posters.
- A title without a backdrop yet shows its poster until VesperTV gets one from your provider. Backdrops are fetched only for the cards on screen and saved, so they're there next time.
- Phones held upright keep the posters.

### ✨ Vesper AI: Sports Trak game cards
- An AI app (such as Claude through your connector) can now **save the right channel on your Sports Trak game cards**: "match tonight's NBA games with my League Pass channels". It reads your channel groups and their guide, and saves up to 40 games at once with one OK on the TV. You can still change any card yourself.
- Its sports answers now say each channel's **picture quality** (4K / FHD / HD / SD), what that channel shows **at the game's start time**, and whether a game hasn't started, is live or is over.
- Tip: if your AI app doesn't see the new abilities, reconnect the VesperTV connector in the app (it keeps an old list).

### ✨ Vesper AI: profiles and parental controls
- Ask it to list, switch, create, rename or delete **profiles**, make a **kids profile** and choose which groups a kids profile can see ("only the kids channels").
- Your **PIN is only ever typed on the TV**: the AI never sees it, and there is no way for it to remove the PIN. Anything that loosens a kids profile (allowing groups, deleting it, a new PIN) asks for the PIN on the TV, and on a kids profile every change does.

### Live TV guide
- Event channels with no guide from the provider (League Pass and similar) now say **"No game at this time"** before and after the game instead of an empty row.

Install:
- **Android**: Downloader code **1760256**, or update from Settings > Check for updates.
- **Windows / Linux**: stay on 1.0.61 (download it from the 1.0.61 release).

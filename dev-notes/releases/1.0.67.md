## VesperTV 1.0.67 (beta)

Android TV, Fire TV and Android phones - build 781. Windows stays on 1.0.61 and Linux on 1.0.63 (build 702) for now.

### ✨ Smoother full screen
- **The glide between the guide and full screen** now works like the best TV apps: going back, the guide is around the picture from the first frame (before, black showed around the shrinking video), and going into full screen the picture starts with a big step and settles in.
- On weaker boxes the guide no longer freezes for a second after OK or Back: the Timeline guide keeps the rows that haven't changed instead of redrawing all of them. Measured on a Homatics box: the pause before the glide back went from about 1 second to a quarter of a second.
- Boxes with Remote buttons (or a launcher such as Monet) turned on no longer pay for an accessibility tree nobody reads. If a screen reader such as TalkBack is on, nothing changes for it.
- Settings, Multiview, Sports Trak, Media Hub and the title pages are lighter: less redrawing while you move between cards, quicker Up / Down in Media Hub.
- Coming back from full screen keeps you in the group you were in (a favourite opened from one of your own groups used to land on Favourites).

### ✨ Movies and TV shows
- **Hold OK on a movie or show poster** now opens a small menu: add to or remove from Favourites, **Mark as watched / unwatched** for a movie, **Mark every episode watched / unwatched** for a show. Works on the Movies and TV Shows rows, Expand catalog, Search and Media Hub, and is sent to Trakt / Simkl when the title has an id.
- **Media Hub player**: press OK and the show name and episode name appear at the top left, beside the source and file size.
- Automatic trailers play on every Android box (on some, the page stayed on its spinner while the trailer's sound played).

### ✨ Phones
- **Home** is the first tab of the bottom bar in portrait, with a round menu button for Settings, Multiview, Update Content and Exit. Home no longer has the big hero on a phone, in portrait or on its side, and its rows start closer to the edge.
- A phone on its side shows the mini player in the profiles row while a channel plays, with a button to turn it off.
- Turning a phone to portrait on Home no longer shows an empty page.

### ✨ Sports Trak
- **The next three days** (Tomorrow and the two after it) now show as rows of their own under Later today, loaded after today's games. Picking another day in the menu shows that day only.
- The day strip and the tools (live count, day menu, Standings, My Teams, Networks) are one row, and moving between cards is smoother.

### ✨ Startup and settings
- **Ask before exiting** (Settings > Profiles & startup > Startup, Android, off by default): Back at the root asks Exit or Stay, for viewers who leave the app with one Back too many.
- Group Management search now finds a word anywhere in a group's name ("ent" finds "US| Entertainment").
- **Sync** now works across 4 devices (was 3).
- Remote buttons set to **Fast forward / Rewind** also jump 10 rows in the groups and channel lists, outside the player.

### Fixes
- Home: the profile strip no longer stays big after Up then Down; Who's watching opens faster.
- Media Hub: the eye on a row no longer toggles twice; an add-on request that failed on a bad connection is asked once more (Continue watching from the launcher sometimes said "No playable stream" right after playing).

Install:
- **Android**: Downloader code **1760256**, or update from Settings > Check for updates.
- **Windows**: stays on 1.0.61 (download it from the 1.0.61 release). **Linux**: 1.0.63, build 702 (from the 1.0.63 release).

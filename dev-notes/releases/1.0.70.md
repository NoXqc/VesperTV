## VesperTV 1.0.70 (beta)

Android TV, Fire TV and Android phones - build 861. **Windows stays on 1.0.68** (no new Windows build); Linux stays on 1.0.63 (build 702).

### ✨ Hiding groups is quicker
- **Group management** (Settings): shown groups are listed first and hidden ones after them, dimmed. New **All / Shown / Hidden** buttons, and the count says how many are hidden.
- **Keep only one country**: Countries now starts with **Hide every group**. Press it, then pick UK (or any country) to show only its groups.
- **Playlist editor** (phone / PC), Groups tab: hidden groups fold away under **Hidden groups** at the bottom, so you no longer scroll past thousands of them. New **Tick listed**, **Hide ticked** and **Show ticked** buttons, a Shown / Hidden filter, **country chips** and **Keep only these countries**.

### ✨ Dispatcharr
- Settings > Playlists & sources > **Dispatcharr**: connect your server (user name and password, the API key, or From phone). **Add as a playlist** fills Add Playlist in for you.
- The live player's **Sources** lists the playing channel's Dispatcharr streams with their provider and measured quality. A **Sources** option picks Dispatcharr first, only Dispatcharr, or only VesperTV. Dispatcharr now shows your user name instead of Anonymous.

### ✨ Live TV
- **Select…** (hold OK on a channel): tick several channels in the list or guide, then add them to Favourites or to a group in one go.
- **Subscription ending reminder**: a card once a day when an Xtream playlist ends within 7 days.
- The live channel plays again when you come back from Media Hub, Home, Movies / TV Shows or Multiview.
- Faster guide on big playlists: changing group and opening Favourites no longer stall while movies and shows load.

### 🐞 Fixes
- Picking a stream in the live player's **Sources** now really switches to it.
- Copies of a channel from different providers match better (QC / CA / Québec, accents), so Sources offers them.
- Right from a group in the list guide always lands on a visible channel.
- Floating Settings panels are smoother on weak boxes.

### ⚽ Sports Trak
- **This game** (Left in the player during a game): **Choose a different channel** when the matched channels are wrong.
- New leagues: Ekstraklasa, Polish I liga, Swiss Super League, Eerste Divisie, League of Ireland.
- Guide titles in team codes ("TOR @ BOS") are found, with a team-codes chip in the channel search.

### ✨ Multiview
- Screens start after about 1 second of video, the channel picker shows each channel's resolution, and tile borders are thinner and lighter.

Install:
- **Android**: Downloader code **1760256**, or update from Settings > Check for updates.
- **Windows**: 1.0.68 (from the 1.0.68 release).
- **Linux**: 1.0.63, build 702 (from the 1.0.63 release).

## VesperTV 1.0.65 (beta)

Android TV, Fire TV and Android phones - build 732. Windows stays on 1.0.61 and Linux on 1.0.63 (build 702) for now.

### ✨ Media Hub collections
- Rows of tiles like Nuvio's: **Settings > Media Hub sources > Collections**. Add a Nuvio collections file from a link or from your phone, or pick **Nuvio Perfect Collections** (it starts with only Streaming on).
- Tiles (Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video...) come alive when focused. A tile opens a page of its catalogs from your own add-ons (AIOMetadata), and a poster you rest on for 2 seconds opens wide.
- Turn a collection on or off, **Show on Home**, move it, update it from its link or remove it. Adding a removed one again brings back its switches. Hold OK on a tile for the same choices.
- The catalogs inside collections leave the Media Hub rows (switch: **Hide their catalogs**), and catalogs an add-on keeps off the home screen only appear in tiles, so the tab stays tidy.
- Streaming services show their own logo in their colour at the top (red NETFLIX).
- A tile whose catalogs aren't in your add-ons says so: add those catalogs to your AIOMetadata, then refresh the Media Hub.

### ✨ Settings, new look
- Settings uses the app's floating glass style. Most one-switch pages are now rows with a short help line.
- **Media Hub sources** is one add-on list like Stremio and Nuvio, with **Manage from phone**: scan the QR to turn add-ons on or off, remove one, copy its link or add a new one from your phone.
- **Group Management > Countries**: show or hide every group of a country at once (US:, UK|, FR...).
- Media Hub: the eye beside Continue watching / Recently watched folds the row down to one line.
- Customize Media Hub: Left / Right reach every button of a row (hide, pin, up, down, rename).

### ✨ Player
- The picture button now cycles **Fit, Stretched, Crop, Fit height, Fit width, Slight zoom and Cinema zoom** (Android). Your choice is remembered.
- 4K films in the IMAX ratio now show the **4K** badge (they showed FHD).

### ✨ Playlists
- Playlist editor: tick channels, then **Move to group...**, **Copy to group...** or **Reset group**. The destination can be one of your own groups or a group of any playlist.
- Xtream: a server typed without http:// now works.

### ✨ Sports Trak
- **EuroCup** and **Basketball Champions League** added.
- When ESPN asks apps to slow down, scores pause for a moment and the screen says so, instead of "check your connection".
- Phones: holding a league chip shows a bar to move it. The game menu names the playlist when a game's channels come from several playlists.

### ✨ Vesper AI apps
- Two new tools: **collections** (turn collections on or off, put one on Home, reorder, add or remove catalogs on a tile, make new tiles) and **hub_rows** (hide, show, pin, move or rename Media Hub rows). Reconnect the VesperTV connector in your AI app to see them.

### Fixes
- Add-ons that label their catalogs "movies" or "shows" now show them under Movies and TV Shows.
- Home: Left / Right work right away after backing out of a page.
- Credits: Settings, the README and the site say ESPN, TheSportsDB and IntroDB (and the leagues and teams shown) are not affiliated with VesperTV.

Install:
- **Android**: Downloader code **1760256**, or update from Settings > Check for updates.
- **Windows**: stays on 1.0.61 (download it from the 1.0.61 release). **Linux**: 1.0.63, build 702 (from the 1.0.63 release).

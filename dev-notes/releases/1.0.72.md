## VesperTV 1.0.72 (beta)

Android TV, Fire TV, Android phones and **Windows** - build 928. (Linux stays at the 1.0.70 build 869 attached to this release.)

### 🐞 Sync - please update every device that syncs
- **Fixed:** since 1.0.71, a device linked to a sync never received your playlists, settings and playlist editor changes (with Fast startup on, the default), and if it already had the same playlist it received nothing at all. Editing favourites or groups on that device could then remove them on your other devices. Now everything arrives when the linked device restarts. The first start after linking takes a few seconds longer while it writes your setup.
- **Scan to link:** Settings > Backup & devices > Sync > Link a device shows a QR code ("Sync a phone? Scan the QR code"). Point an Android phone's camera at it: VesperTV opens with the code filled in, press **Join sync**. A new phone skips the "Add your first playlist?" step.
- **Dispatcharr** and **Tuliprox** sign-ins now come along with Transfer, Sync and backups. Restoring an older backup that doesn't have them keeps the device's own sign-in.

### ✨ Live TV guide
- **Long channel names on two lines** (new, Settings > Interface > Guide, off by default): channel names that don't fit go on a second line.
- **Programme details**: in the full-video guide, rest on a programme for 1 second to see its description under the row (Settings > Interface > Guide > Programme details in the guide). The full-video guide now reaches the edges of the screen.
- **Channel numbers** switch (Settings > Interface > Guide), and with nothing playing the guide preview shows the highlighted channel's logo.
- **Settings** is in the guide's hold-OK menus, and **Update Content** now refreshes the TV guide too.

### ✨ Playlist editor
- **Link guide…** for every ticked channel at once, and **Channel order** for a group (custom groups too) from the Groups tab.

### ✨ Media Hub
- **IPTV copies in Sources** (new, Settings > Player > Movies & TV Shows, off by default): a Media Hub movie or episode also lists the same title from your IPTV playlists in its Sources.
- **Phones**: one top row and the bottom bar with Movies / TV Shows / Sources / Sort / Discover.

### ✨ More
- **Ukrainian** language.
- Backup & restore > **How often**: every 1, 3, 7, 14 or 30 days.
- Movie and show pages: a focused button no longer pushes its neighbour to the next line (Watch list was unreachable on Fire TV in German).

Install:
- **Android**: Downloader code **1760256**, or update from Settings > Check for updates.
- **Windows**: `VesperTV-windows-x64.zip` from this release (unzip, run `VesperTV.exe`), or Settings > Check for updates.
- **Linux** (beta, x64): `VesperTV-1.0.70-869-linux-x64.tar.gz` from this release is the 1.0.70 build (needs `libmpv2`; extract, run `./vespertv`); it doesn't have the changes above yet.

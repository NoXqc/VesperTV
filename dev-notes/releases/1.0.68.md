## VesperTV 1.0.68 (beta)

Android TV, Fire TV and Android phones - build 802. **Windows is back in step** (the same 1.0.68 build); Linux stays on 1.0.63 (build 702).

### ✨ Minimalist
- **One switch for a plain, TiviMate-like player** (Settings > Appearance > Minimalist): the classic compact Timeline guide, groups that fold away, no Home, profiles row, Sports Trak or Media Hub, and it opens on TV. Switching it off puts back exactly what you had; each part is still its own setting.
- **Hide the Home tab** on its own (Settings > Appearance > Tabs > Home).

### ✨ Live TV
- The channel rows in the live list no longer carry an eye and a star: **hold OK** on a channel for Favourite and Hide (a long press on a touch screen opens the same menu).
- The channel info card no longer covers the channel list when the groups column is open.
- The Timeline guide **follows the clock** while it sits at live, and opens a new group at live.

### ✨ Media Hub
- **Expand a collection**, switch between Movies and TV Shows, "Coming soon" and NEW tags, and a **Sort and Filter** for the grid (it no longer freezes on big libraries).
- Multiview can show **recordings** beside live channels.
- A Jellyfin-style **AIOStreams** server: libraries and collections show up as a Media Hub server.

### ✨ Sports Trak
- **Sports Mode** lists the next three days under Today; the feed beside the video shows My Teams and Live now only.

### ✨ Windows
- **Stop** in full screen (and in the guide preview) really stops the channel. It used to leave the sound playing with no picture, one more for every channel picked afterwards.
- Mouse wheel and trackpad scroll the guide's channel list; Movies, TV Shows and Media Hub stop the live channel (it comes back when you return to TV); Sports Mode follows, previews and has an Exit button; bigger Search results that follow Interface size; more player keys.
- Esc / Backspace go Back, zapping works, groups can be browsed, and the menus draw inside the app window.
- One exit: the sidebar's duplicate **Close** is gone, **Exit VesperTV** stays.

### ✨ Smoother menus
- Hold menus use a frozen blur and their own layers, and the Home backdrop cuts instead of fading under the mini player: no more half-drawn frames on weak boxes at 4K HDR.

Install:
- **Android**: Downloader code **1760256**, or update from Settings > Check for updates.
- **Windows**: `VesperTV-windows-x64.zip` below (unzip the whole folder, then run `VesperTV.exe`).
- **Linux**: 1.0.63, build 702 (from the 1.0.63 release).

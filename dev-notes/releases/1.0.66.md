## VesperTV 1.0.66 (beta)

Android TV, Fire TV and Android phones - build 753. Windows stays on 1.0.61 and Linux on 1.0.63 (build 702) for now.

### ✨ Remote buttons
- **Settings > Backup & devices > Remote buttons**: give your remote's spare buttons a job in VesperTV, a short press and a hold each (Live guide, Favourites, Multiview, Play / Pause, Record, Next audio track and more). You can also choose what Left / Right / Up / Down do in the full-screen player. Per profile.
- New **Rewind** and **Fast forward** actions: the player's own steps, and back into catch-up on live channels.
- **Buttons your box keeps**: some boxes keep buttons for themselves (on Homatics: TV, YouTube, Prime, the app button and Settings). Turn on "VesperTV remote buttons" in Android's Accessibility settings (the Remote buttons page takes you there) and VesperTV can use them while it is on screen. Outside VesperTV they keep their usual job. The TV / Guide button then opens the live guide. Netflix buttons stay out of reach.
- VesperTV isn't installed from a store, so Android 13 and newer may first ask you to allow restricted settings in VesperTV's App info. The Remote buttons page has a shortcut to it.

### ✨ Interface
- **Interface size** (Settings > Appearance > Look): Compact, Normal or Large. Large has wider columns, taller rows and bigger text, for easier reading from the couch. Per profile; it replaces the Compact UI switch.
- TV boxes: a column you have moved past (tabs, then groups) hides completely, so the next one gets the room. Sort groups moved to the top bar, above the groups column.
- **Clock in full screen** (Settings > Appearance > Language and clock): a small see-through clock in the top-right corner of the full-screen player.
- **Polish** language.
- New launcher icon and Android TV banner from the gold logo.

### ✨ Smoother on weaker boxes
- The Live TV home, groups, channel list and Timeline guide are much smoother on weaker boxes: nothing is redrawn while nothing changes, changing group no longer stalls for a second, channel logos load at the size they show, and columns slide without redrawing the guide on every frame.
- Channel logos in the plain channel list are no longer squeezed into squares.

### ✨ Live TV
- **Picture subtitles**: DVB subtitles (European channels), PGS and VobSub tracks now show in the Subtitles menu and on screen.
- Coming back to VesperTV resumes the channel you were watching. After zapping, it could come back on an earlier channel.

### ✨ Media Hub
- **Automatically play the largest file** no longer picks a whole-season pack for one episode (a 45 GB "Season 2" link used to beat the 13 GB 4K episode).

### ✨ Sports Trak
- **Goals alert delay** (Settings > Sports Trak): no delay, 15, 30 or 60 seconds, so the goal popup doesn't arrive before you see the goal on a stream that runs behind.

### ✨ Vesper AI apps
- New **epg_matching** tool: paste an XMLTV guide link (or name another playlist) and your AI app matches the channels that have no guide, the sure ones first. Every change can be undone. Reconnect the VesperTV connector in your AI app to see it.

### Backup & transfer
- Transfer and Backup now also carry Continue Watching files and Recording settings (network folder, download speed and time, margins), and playback stops before an import.

### Fixes
- Changing channel with the native video surface: the cover shows a spinner only, no app icon.
- Customize Media Hub: a moved row keeps its focus outline. The player's options row lines up again when Buffer is Off.

Install:
- **Android**: Downloader code **1760256**, or update from Settings > Check for updates.
- **Windows**: stays on 1.0.61 (download it from the 1.0.61 release). **Linux**: 1.0.63, build 702 (from the 1.0.63 release).

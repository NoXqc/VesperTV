## VesperTV 1.0.50 (beta)

Android TV, Fire TV and Android phones - build 534

### Media Hub and IPTV
- **Plex support:** connect your own Plex server from the shared Jellyfin / Emby / Plex connector. Approve sign-in with a code or QR, then browse its movie and TV libraries and play original files.
- **Server files from tracker lists:** titles opened from Simkl, Trakt or compatible catalog lists now find matching files on connected Plex, Jellyfin and Emby servers.
- **IPTV actor portraits:** add your own TMDB key to see cast portraits and actor credits on IPTV movie and show pages. Quality prefixes such as 4K: no longer obstruct title lookup.
- **Customize title pages:** choose the order of cast, related titles and collections, including putting actors first.

### Android playback
- **Skip intro, recap and credits:** episode buttons appear when timings are available from IntroDB. Credits can offer the next episode. Enabled by default; change it under Settings > Playback > Skip intro. IPTV shows can use the viewer's TMDB key when provider IDs are missing. Press Up from the player bar to reach the skip button on a remote.
- **Phone touch lock:** tap the open padlock in the player bar to block accidental taps, swipes and Back while video continues. Tap the visible closed padlock to unlock.

### Sports Trak
- **Channel preferences:** Settings > Sports Trak adds preferred channel countries and a preferred playlist. Other matching channels remain available, and saved channel choices retain priority.
- **My Teams:** hold a team to set its preferred channel, even when it has no game today.
- **Hide empty leagues:** optionally hide leagues with no games on the selected day; saved leagues remain in Edit leagues.
- **Cleaner league bar:** short labels such as AHL, EPL, UCL and F1 save space. Full names remain in Edit leagues. Prefer highest quality moves to Settings > Sports Trak with your saved choice retained.
- **Betting odds:** optional lines and over/under appear where the sports feed supplies them. Off by default, under Settings > Sports Trak > Betting odds. Display only.

### Updates
- Android checks free storage before downloading an update, verifies the downloaded APK size, and clears the previous cached installer at the next launch. New messages include French, Spanish, Italian and Greek translations.

### Windows
Windows remains at **1.0.45**. This release contains an Android APK only; Windows has not been rebuilt or tested for these changes. Its existing download remains available on the website and the 1.0.45 release.

### Install
Update through Settings > Check for Updates, download app-release.apk, or use Downloader code **1760256**. Install over your existing app to keep your setup.

Connect Claude or ChatGPT

Link your AI app to VesperTV once, then ask in plain words from your phone or computer: “play the Canadiens game”, “what's on TSN 1?”, “make a kids profile for Emma”. It acts on your TV right away.

Three ways to use Vesper AI

Built-in commands

Open Vesper in the side menu and type or speak: “play TSN 1”, “pause”, “today's games”. Free, on your device, no account.

Your own AI key

Settings → Vesper AI → Your AI: paste a Claude, OpenAI-compatible or Gemini key to talk to Vesper on the TV with a full AI.

This page

AI apps

Your Claude or ChatGPT app (phone, computer, web) controls the TV through a private connector address. Uses your existing AI app, no key to paste.

1. On the TV: get your address

  1. Open Settings → Vesper AI → AI apps.
  2. Turn on Let AI apps control VesperTV. A QR code and an address starting with https://pair.vespertv.app/mcp/ appear, with Connected to the relay in green.
  3. Scan the QR code with your phone. It opens your Vesper AI link page: press Copy. (On a computer, you can also type the address shown on the TV.)

Keep this address private. Anyone with it can control VesperTV while it is open. If it leaks, press Reset the address on the same screen: the old one stops working and you add the new one to your app.

2. Add it to your AI app

Claude

claude.ai on the web, or the Claude app on your phone or computer.

  1. Open Settings → Connectors and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Fill it in:
    Name
    VesperTV
    URL
    paste your address
    then press Add.
  3. If Claude asks you to Connect or sign in, go ahead: VesperTV approves it at once, there is no account or password.
  4. In a chat, open the tools menu under the message box and make sure VesperTV is turned on. Then just ask.

ChatGPT

chatgpt.com in a browser (the custom server option may not be in every plan or app yet).

  1. Go to chatgpt.com/plugins, press Add and choose Add custom MCP server.
  2. Fill it in:
    Name
    VesperTV
    Description
    optional
    Connection
    Server URL: paste your address
    Authentication
    leave OAuth (VesperTV approves it at once)
    then create it.
  3. Older layout: Settings → Connectors (with developer mode on) → Create, paste the address, no authentication.
  4. In a chat, add VesperTV from the tools / plugins menu, then ask.

Any other AI app that supports MCP: add a Streamable HTTP server at your address.

3. Ask away

  • “What's on TSN 1 right now?”Guide, now and next
  • “Play the Leafs game in French.”Finds the channel carrying it
  • “Match tonight's NBA games with my League Pass channels and save them on the cards.”Sports Trak game cards, 1.0.62+
  • “Make a kids profile for Emma that only sees the kids channels.”Profiles & parental, 1.0.62+
  • “Hide every group with Turkish in its name.”Groups
  • “Turn off skip intro and set subtitles to large.”Settings
  • “Add Dune to my watch list.”Watch list on Home
  • “Show ‘Dinner is ready!’ on the TV.”A message on screen

What the TV asks before it acts

  1. Ask on the TV first (Settings → Vesper AI → AI apps, on by default): playing, hiding, changing settings or clearing history from an AI app waits for OK on the TV. Turn it off and your AI app acts right away. It only concerns AI apps: the built-in commands and your own AI key never ask.
  2. Your parental PIN is only ever typed on the TV. The AI never sees it and can't remove it. Allowing groups to a kids profile, deleting one or changing the PIN shows the PIN pad on the TV, even with Ask on the TV first off.
  3. Deleting a profile always asks on the TV.

Not working?

“The TV is offline” or the app can't reach VesperTV
VesperTV must be open on the TV, with Let AI apps control VesperTV on and Connected to the relay showing. A box asleep or in standby can't answer.
My AI app says it can't do something new (game cards, profiles…)
AI apps keep the list of VesperTV abilities they saw when you connected. After a VesperTV update, disconnect and reconnect the VesperTV connector (or remove it and add it again), then start a new chat.
Nothing happens after I ask
With Ask on the TV first on, the TV is waiting for OK. Nobody answers within a minute = no.
Several TVs?
Each TV has its own address. Add each one as its own connector (“VesperTV living room”, “VesperTV bedroom”).
Is my data stored somewhere?
No. Requests pass through the VesperTV relay to your TV while the app is open; the relay only holds them in memory on the way and saves nothing. What you say to Claude or ChatGPT is handled by that app, under its own terms.

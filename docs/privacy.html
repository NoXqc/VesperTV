Privacy Policy

VesperTV is a free, open-source media player for Android, Fire TV, Windows, macOS, Linux, iPhone and iPad. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. This page explains what stays on your device and the few things that leave it, only when you use the feature that needs it.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

In short: we don't collect, sell or share personal data. There is no VesperTV account, and the app has no analytics or advertising code. Your playlists, history and settings live on your device.

What stays on your device

Everything you set up in VesperTV is stored only on the device you use it on: playlists and their login details, Media Hub sources, the TV guide, favourites, watch history and resume positions, viewer profiles and photos, Sports Trak teams, recordings, downloads, backups and settings. We never receive any of it. Uninstalling the app deletes it, except backups you saved to a folder of your choice.

Services the app talks to

VesperTV ships no content. It connects to the services you add or turn on, and they receive the usual information any internet request carries (such as your IP address) under their own privacy policies:

  • Your own sources: the IPTV providers, add-ons and media servers (Jellyfin, Emby, Plex) you add, with the logins you enter.
  • Sports Trak: public score and schedule feeds (ESPN, TheSportsDB). No personal data is sent.
  • Artwork and details: Cinemeta, TMDB (with your own key, if you add one) and IntroDB for skip-intro times.
  • Trakt and Simkl: only if you sign in, to sync what you watch with your account.
  • Trailers: played from YouTube when you open one.
  • Your AI provider: if you add your own key under Vesper AI, your requests go straight from the app to that provider (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or another you choose).
  • Updates: Android and Windows check GitHub for a new version. Apple devices update through the App Store.

The VesperTV relay (only when you use these features)

A small server at pair.vespertv.app makes a few features work across devices. It keeps no access logs and stores no IP addresses.

  • Transfer to another device and Sync: your setup is encrypted on your device before it is uploaded. The server only holds the encrypted data and cannot read it. A transfer is deleted when it expires (1 hour to 7 days, your choice) or when you delete it. A sync is deleted after 3 months without any of its devices checking in.
  • Add from phone: what you type on your phone (a playlist link, a key) passes through the relay to your TV and is not kept.
  • AI apps (Vesper AI): requests from an AI app you link pass through the relay to your device while the app is open. They are held in memory on the way and never saved. What you say to the AI app is handled by that app, under its own terms.
  • Send log: only when you press Send in Settings, a diagnostic log goes to the developer to fix a bug. Addresses, usernames and passwords are removed from it first, and you see it before sending.

Voice, microphone and local network

Voice commands use your device's own speech recognition only while you hold the mic or press the voice key. On some devices that service is run by the device maker (Apple or Google) under their terms. Local network access is used only to reach your own media server and to copy a setup between your devices on the same Wi-Fi.

Children

VesperTV collects no personal data from anyone, including children. Parents can use viewer profiles with a PIN to limit what a child sees.

Your choices

Every feature that connects to the relay or an outside account is optional and off until you use it. You can delete a transfer or a sync from the app, sign out of Trakt or Simkl, remove your keys, and delete all of your data by uninstalling VesperTV.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date. The app's source code is public on GitHub, so anyone can check what it does. Questions: open an issue on GitHub or ask on our Discord.

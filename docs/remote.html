Remote tips

Everything the remote can do that isn't written on screen. Pick the screen you're on, or search for what you want to do ("move a channel", "record", "subtitles").

Hold OKHold = menu

Press OK and keep it down for about half a second. Almost everything has a menu behind it: channels, groups, posters, games, screens. Phone: long-press. Mouse: long-press too.

BackBack is always safe

Back steps out one level at a time: the list, then the groups, then the tabs. The app only closes when you press Back on the tabs column.

OK OKOK twice = full screen

On a channel, the first OK plays it in the preview box and the second goes full screen. On a text box, OK opens the keyboard; otherwise the arrows just pass over it.

Nothing matches. Try another word, or ask on Discord.

Live TV: channel list and guide

The TV tab, its channel list and the Timeline guide. Timeline is the default on new installs; a saved layout choice is kept.

On Android, Settings → Theme → Live TV guide → Timeline → Full-screen video behind the guide: enable Expand to fill the screen with guide rows. Turn it off to restore the three-row strip.

Android · Timeline · ← into the past · OK twice
Watch catch-up TV

On a channel where your provider offers catch-up, open the Timeline guide and press Left to browse earlier programmes. Available replays have a gold ↺ mark. OK on a past programme starts the preview; OK again on that programme opens it full screen. Older hours without programme names appear as Catch-up HH:MM blocks and can also be played. How far back you can watch depends on your provider's retention window.

In the guide: Back or the gold Now button
Back to now in the guide

When browsing a different time in the Timeline guide, Back returns the guide to now; the gold Now button does the same. This only moves the guide: a replay keeps playing. Fast-forward does not return to live from the guide. To change playback back to live, open the replay full screen and use its gold LIVE button or the remote's Fast-forward key. Back from full screen returns to the guide while the replay continues.

Update Content → update playlists · Settings → Playlists & guide → EPG → Update EPG Now
Catch-up not working

If replay channels or past programmes are missing after updating, run Update Content and update your playlists, then Update EPG Now once. Catch-up is available on Android TV, Fire TV and Android phones; Windows support is not available yet. Your provider must offer catch-up for that channel. The provider decides how many days are available; guide data may cover fewer hours, with older hours shown as Catch-up HH:MM blocks. Refreshing cannot add replay support to a channel your provider does not support.

OK then OK
Preview, then full screen

The first OK plays the channel in the preview box; OK again opens it full screen.

Search (magnifying glass) in the side menu
Search channels

Open Search from the side menu and enter the channel name. Matching channels appear in the Live TV row. Move to a channel and press OK to play it. Search also shows matching movies, TV shows and Media Hub titles in separate rows.

Hold OK on a channel
Channel menu

Add to / Remove from Favourites, Hide this channel, Move Up / Down (your own order inside the group or Favourites), Move to group…, Remove from a group, and Record on Android. To reorder Favourites in the list or Timeline guide, hold OK on a channel, choose Move Up / Down, use ↑ / ↓, then OK or Back to finish. Each viewer's order is saved across playlists and takes priority over the default playlist or A-Z order. Clear the guide's search filter before moving a channel.

The filter icon offers Provider order, Name A–Z and Name Z–A. Each playlist stays together; your own groups sort separately. The choice is remembered on this device. Manual Move Up / Down or Move to Top restores the stored order. Favourites channel order stays as you arranged it.

Resume the last channel

What the "Hold > to resume" pill means. Works even with the pill turned off in Settings.

Settings → Interface → Theme → Refined finish
Refine the existing appearance

Off by default and independent for each profile. Adds glossy panels, brighter reflections, bevelled edges and blur around focused controls. Uses more graphics resources. Keeps the same layout, theme colours, text sizes and remote navigation. Turn it off to restore the original finish.

Settings → Interface → Resume reminder
Hide the resume reminder

Turn off Show the "Hold > to resume" reminder. This hides the pill while a channel plays in the background. On Android, holding Right still resumes the last channel.

Full-screen player

While a channel, film or episode plays full screen.

Android: Settings → Playback → Audio has preferred audio and subtitle languages for movies and episodes. Manual audio/subtitle choices are remembered for that series or movie in your profile and take priority on Resume, including subtitles Off. Quick way for subtitles: in the player, Subtitles › Preferred subtitle language. When the file has no track in that language, your subtitle add-ons (Settings → Add-ons, e.g. OpenSubtitles) supply it, for IPTV movies and Media Hub titles alike. The source default audio remains when there is no match.

Android phones and tablets (Android 8+ with picture-in-picture support): while video plays full screen, press Home or swipe home to keep watching in a floating window. Tap the window to return; close it to pause. Paused playback and other app screens do not start picture-in-picture. Android may require allowing picture-in-picture in its per-app settings.

Android replay · gold LIVE button or remote ⏩ Fast-forward
Back to live

While a catch-up replay plays full screen, select the gold LIVE button to return to the channel live. The remote's dedicated Fast-forward key does the same in full screen. It is not a guide shortcut: Back or Now in the guide only moves the guide to the current time and leaves the replay playing.

Android · player bar: Watch from the start · remote ⏪ Rewind
Watch a programme from the start

To restart a programme on a catch-up-capable channel, open the player bar and choose Watch from the start, or press the remote's dedicated Rewind key in full screen. On live TV this starts the programme on now from its beginning. During a replay it restarts the programme at your current playback time. Your provider must offer that part of the programme.

Android catch-up channel · remote ⏯ or player Play/Pause
Pause live TV and resume

You can pause live TV on a channel that supports catch-up, then press Play to resume from where you paused. Short pauses use the existing buffer; after more than 90 seconds VesperTV uses the provider's catch-up feed (timeshift). Availability still depends on the provider's replay window. Use the gold LIVE button in full screen when you want to return to live.

Android replay · player bar: previous / restart / next · ↑ to the time bar
Seek and change replay programmes

The catch-up player shows a CATCH-UP tag, programme progress and how far behind live you are. Use the time bar to seek within the programme, or the previous / next programme buttons when listings are available. Next returns to live if there is no later programme to replay. At the end of the programme you picked, playback pauses so the next one doesn't start on its own: press Play to carry on, or turn on Settings → Playback → Catch-up → Keep playing after the programme. A paused live channel resuming through catch-up keeps going until it catches up to live.

Media Hub sources → ↑ / ↓ / ← / →
Manage source cards

Open from Settings › Media Hub › Media Hub sources, or Sources in the Media Hub tab. Add a source, From phone, Stremio import, Jellyfin / Emby and Trakt / Simkl are at the top. Playback sources provide video; Catalogs & lists, including Cinemeta, provide titles that play through those sources. Hold OK on any upcoming Sports Trak game
Remind me

Choose Remind me to open the timing menu: 15 minutes, 1 hour, 1 day or 1 week before, plus a reminder at game time. You can also choose At game time only. Passed advance times are unavailable. Popups appear above any screen or stream while VesperTV is open. You do not need to follow either team. For teams in My Teams, choose Remind me for every game to enable automatic reminders for that team, or Stop team reminders to disable them. Hold again to Cancel reminder for one game, or choose Reminder timing to change its advance time. Automatic team reminders keep the 15-minute default unless you override an individual game. A faded team logo appears behind the popup text. Reminders belong to the current viewer on this device.

Hold OK on an upcoming or live team game
Score alerts for one match

Choose Set goals alert for this match to get the existing top-left score popup when either team scores, even if neither team is in My Teams. Remove goals alert for this match stops it. This per-game choice works independently of the My Teams Goals alert switch. Hide scores still pauses all score alerts. Choices belong to this profile and device; alerts follow the sports feed while VesperTV is open.

Settings → Interface → Goals alert
Score alerts for My Teams

Off by default. Enable it for a top-left popup when any followed team scores, above any screen or stream while VesperTV is open. The card shows the team and league logos and the current score. Game reminders stay top-right; on a phone the cards stack. Alerts follow the sports feed's update speed, pause with Hide scores or when the app is in the background, and do not replay old scores on launch or profile changes.

Hold OK on a team in Standings or My Teams

Football (soccer) includes the cups (FA Cup, Carabao Cup, Champions League…), marked with the cup's logo. Arrows move a day or a week, CH+/CH- change month. OK shows the stats of a finished game, watches a live one or opens the game menu of an upcoming one (reminder, record, channel); hold OK opens the game menu.

Sports Trak Networks

Networks sits beside Standings and My Teams. Each card is one network (TSN, ESPN, Sportsnet…) with all of its channels. OK previews it at the top; OK again opens full screen. Hold OK to pick another of its channels, favourite the network (Favourite networks row) or add the channel to a list. Back returns to Games, and a game that was playing keeps playing there. Create list lets you pick channels yourself or use a playlist group, even without guide data. Filters include Unknown and support multiple choices. In a game menu, Add sources saves your choices for the game, team or league; More sources shows further matching feeds and versions.

Trailers

On IPTV and Media Hub movie or show details, Trailer appears when supplied metadata or your installed add-ons provide a trailer. YouTube trailers play inside VesperTV: OK plays or pauses, Left/Right skip 10 seconds and Back closes. Videos that disallow embedding open in YouTube; other errors offer the same option. Direct media trailers use the normal player. Returning does not start the movie or mark it watched.

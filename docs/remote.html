Remote tips

Everything the remote can do that isn't written on screen. Pick the screen you're on, or search for what you want to do ("move a channel", "record", "subtitles").

Hold OKHold = menu

Press OK and keep it down for about half a second. Almost everything has a menu behind it: channels, groups, posters, games, screens. Phone: long-press. Mouse: long-press too.

BackBack is always safe

Back steps out one level at a time: the list, then the groups, then the tabs. The app only closes when you press Back on the tabs column.

OK OKOK twice = full screen

On a channel, the first OK plays it in the preview box and the second goes full screen. On a text box, OK opens the keyboard; otherwise the arrows just pass over it.

Nothing matches. Try another word, or ask on Discord.

Live TV: channel list and guide

The TV tab, its channel list and the Timeline guide. Favourites is selected on a fresh TV launch and when returning from Home, even when no channel is playing or your favourites are empty. Timeline is the default on new installs; a saved layout choice is kept.

On Android, Settings → Theme → Live TV guide → Timeline → Full-screen video behind the guide: enable Expand to fill the screen with guide rows. Turn it off to restore the three-row strip.

Guide / EPGANDROID TV
Guide / EPG button

Press the remote's Guide or EPG button from a screen in your profile to open the live guide on the playing channel. It also works from full screen and supports the Formuler EPG button. In Sports Mode it opens the in-page Live TV panel and keeps the current game playing until you choose another channel. On regular TV home it opens the TV tab and guide. The splash and profile selector keep their normal controls.

Android · Timeline · ← into the past · OK twice
Watch catch-up TV

On a channel where your provider offers catch-up, open the Timeline guide and press Left to browse earlier programmes. Available replays have a gold ↺ mark. OK on a past programme starts the preview; OK again on that programme opens it full screen. Older hours without programme names appear as Catch-up HH:MM blocks and can also be played. How far back you can watch depends on your provider's retention window.

In the guide: Back or the gold Now button
Back to now in the guide

When browsing a different time in the Timeline guide, Back returns the guide to now; the gold Now button does the same. This only moves the guide: a replay keeps playing. Fast-forward does not return to live from the guide. To change playback back to live, open the replay full screen and use its gold LIVE button, or Fast-forward to the end of the replay. Back from full screen returns to the guide while the replay continues.

Update Content → update playlists · Settings → Playlists & guide → EPG → Update EPG Now
Catch-up not working

If replay channels or past programmes are missing after updating, run Update Content and update your playlists, then Update EPG Now once. Catch-up is available on Android TV, Fire TV and Android phones; Windows support is not available yet. Your provider must offer catch-up for that channel. The provider decides how many days are available; guide data may cover fewer hours, with older hours shown as Catch-up HH:MM blocks. Refreshing cannot add replay support to a channel your provider does not support.

Full-screen player

While a channel, film or episode plays full screen.

Android: Settings → Playback → Audio has preferred audio and subtitle languages for movies and episodes. Manual audio/subtitle choices are remembered for that series or movie in your profile and take priority on Resume, including subtitles Off. Quick way for subtitles: in the player, Subtitles › Preferred subtitle language. When the file has no track in that language, your subtitle add-ons (Settings → Add-ons, e.g. OpenSubtitles) supply it, for IPTV movies and Media Hub titles alike. The source default audio remains when there is no match.

Android phones and tablets (Android 8+ with picture-in-picture support): while video plays full screen, press Home or swipe home to keep watching in a floating window. Tap the window to return; close it to pause. Paused playback and other app screens do not start picture-in-picture. Android may require allowing picture-in-picture in its per-app settings.

Android phone / tablet · padlock
Lock touch controls

Tap the full-screen video to show its controls, then tap the open padlock in the top bar. Video keeps playing while taps, swipes and Back are blocked. The closed padlock stays visible on the left: tap it once to unlock and restore the controls. The lock stays through rotation and episode changes, is hidden in picture-in-picture, and resets when you leave the player. Android system Home and volume controls remain available. TV boxes do not show this button.

Android replay · gold LIVE button
Back to live

While a catch-up replay plays full screen, select the gold LIVE button to return to the channel live. Fast-forwarding to the end of the replay also returns to live. It is not a guide shortcut: Back or Now in the guide only moves the guide to the current time and leaves the replay playing.

Android · player bar: Watch from the start
Watch a programme from the start

To restart a programme on a catch-up-capable channel, open the player bar and choose Watch from the start, or open the Channel schedule and pick it. Your provider must offer that part of the programme.

Android · remote ⏪ Rewind / ⏩ Fast-forward
Skip back and forward

In full screen, on a movie, an episode or a catch-up replay, each press skips 10 seconds like the -10 / +10 buttons; keep pressing or hold to skip further (up to 5 minutes a step). The video jumps once you stop. On a live channel with catch-up, Rewind goes back in minutes from now.

Android catch-up channel · remote ⏯ or player Play/Pause
Pause live TV and resume

You can pause live TV on a channel that supports catch-up, then press Play to resume from where you paused. Short pauses use the existing buffer; after more than 90 seconds VesperTV uses the provider's catch-up feed (timeshift). Availability still depends on the provider's replay window. Use the gold LIVE button in full screen when you want to return to live.

Android replay · player bar: previous / restart / next · ↑ to the time bar
Seek and change replay programmes

The catch-up player shows a CATCH-UP tag, programme progress and how far behind live you are. Use the time bar to seek within the programme, or the previous / next programme buttons when listings are available. Next returns to live if there is no later programme to replay. At the end of the programme you picked, playback pauses so the next one doesn't start on its own: press Play to carry on, or turn on Settings → Playback → Catch-up → Keep playing after the programme. A paused live channel resuming through catch-up keeps going until it catches up to live.

Settings → Sports Trak → Phone notifications
Game and goal notifications on Android

Game reminders and Goal alerts are on by default for this viewer; they notify only for reminders and goals you subscribed to. Reminders use the saved advance time and kickoff time even after normal app closure. Vesper AI

Vesper AI is the voice and typed-command assistant in Android 1.0.59+. Built-in commands are free, with no AI account or API key. Vesper AI without an API key

Talk from my phone → scan the QR code → type or use the phone keyboard microphone
Talk to Vesper AI from your phone

Open Talk from my phone in the Vesper panel or Settings → Vesper AI. Scan the QR code with your phone and leave VesperTV open on the TV. Settings → Vesper AI → Remote voice button opens Vesper → Teach it your remote's button

Built-in commands · Your AI · AI apps over the relay
Vesper AI privacy

With Your AI set to None, typed commands are handled without an AI provider. When enabled, Your AI can handle unrecognized requests and failed built-in commands. Sync across your devices

Upcoming release: Settings → Device & data → Sync. No account or login. This feature is not in the current public Android or Windows packages.

Settings > Device & data > Sync > Set up sync
Set up sync without an account

Sync is planned for an upcoming release; it is not in public Android 1.0.56 or Windows 1.0.45 yet. Open Sync on your first device and choose Set up sync. No account, email or login is needed. Save the recovery code shown once, then choose I saved my recovery code. Your setup is encrypted on the device; the server stores only ciphertext and cannot read it.

Sync > Link a device; other device > I have a code
Link another device to sync

On an already linked device, choose Link a device. Enter its 16-digit link code under I have a code on the other device; the QR contains the same code as text. Each link code is single-use: it works once, for one device, and expires after 10 minutes. Its first download uses it up. Create a new link code for each additional device; an expired or already used code needs a new one. Use the Sync code screen, not Transfer > Receive. All link codes and the recovery code share the same limit of 3 devices per sync; another code does not reset that count. If both devices already have the same server and username or M3U URL, sync matches that playlist so favourites and watch positions line up.

Settings > Device & data > Sync
What syncs between devices

Playlists, viewer profiles, favourites and their order, hidden groups and channels, own groups, watch positions, recently played / Continue Watching lists, Sports Trak preferences, Media Hub sources/favourites/resume/history and shared settings sync. Layout, enabled playlists on this box, playlist/guide caches, current viewer, PIN lockout and backup timers stay on this device. Profile photos and imported M3U files do not sync in this first version. Continue Watching and Media Hub Recently watched merge newest first. Sync copies your setup, not channels or video files.

Sync > Sync now
Sync now and last sync time

Last sync shows the time of the latest successful sync; Sync now requests another round. Check the error message if it cannot reach the server. Linked devices also sync while VesperTV is open and when it returns to the screen. Some setup and settings changes appear after a full restart. The first join of an existing playlist can also wait until restart to keep its identity and favourites together.

Sync > Show recovery code; other device > I have a code > Use a recovery code
Show or use a sync recovery code

The recovery code has 26 characters, displayed in groups with dashes, plus a QR. Show recovery code asks for the profile PIN when one is set. On the new device choose I have a code, then Use a recovery code, and enter it. Keep this code private: anyone holding it can join your sync. A 16-digit link code works once for one device; the recovery code is the lasting way back in. Both use the same 3-device limit for that sync.

Keep a linked device or the saved recovery code
Lost sync recovery code

If you lost the recovery code but still have a linked device, open Sync > Show recovery code there (profile PIN when set). If you lose all linked devices and the recovery code, we cannot recover your old sync. Set up a new sync from the data you still have. No email or account can restore the old code.

Sync > Reset sync code
Reset the sync code

Reset sync code asks for the profile PIN when set, then confirmation. Reset removes all your encrypted data from the old server copy and clears its device slots. This device keeps its local data and starts a new sync. Save the new recovery code. Every other device stops syncing and must be linked again using a new link code or the new recovery code. Only a small reset notice remains for 30 days so offline devices can learn of the reset; the old snapshot cannot be uploaded again during that period. Use reset if a code was shared accidentally or a linked box was sold.

Sync > Stop syncing on this device
Stop syncing on this device

Confirm Stop syncing on this device to unlink only this device. Your local setup stays, and other devices keep syncing. If this device can reach the server, stopping also frees its place among the 3 devices. This is different from Reset sync code, which requires other devices to link again.

No device checks in for 3 months
Sync deleted after three months

The server deletes a sync after 3 months (90 days) without any linked device checking in. Using a linked device refreshes that period. The temporary 16-digit link code still expires after 10 minutes. After server expiry, a device with the recovery code can start that sync again from its remaining local data; the deleted server snapshot cannot be recovered.

Sync > Stop syncing on one linked device
Sync device limit: 3 devices

Each sync supports 3 devices in total. Link codes and the recovery code all lead to the same sync, so generating another code does not reset the count. If you see “This sync already has 3 devices”, open Sync on one linked device and choose Stop syncing on this device, then try linking again. A device unused for 30 days frees its place automatically. A device returning after losing its place needs to link again if the sync is full.

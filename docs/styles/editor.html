Live style editor

Draw little pixel sprites, give them a path across a group row, and bring the result into VesperTV. The row below plays it the way your TV will: it loops while the remote is on the group and shows its rest moment when it's not. Your work stays in this browser until you copy or download it. Pause, pick a path point (or a still actor) on the right, then click the row to put it there. On the TV a scene starts at its rest moment, loops while the remote stays on the group (about 30 s, whole loops) and is back at the rest moment when the remote leaves. On a phone it plays for about 12 s when the row shows.

      Bring it into VesperTV

      From your phoneOn the TV: Settings > Appearance > Look > Your live styles > From phone. Scan the code, paste the copied text, send.
      As a linkPut the file online (a GitHub Gist's Raw link works) and paste the link in Your live styles. Changes reach the TV by themselves.
      On a computerOpen VesperTV, Your live styles, paste the text in the box and press Import.

      Then hold OK on a group > Colour… > Live styles… > Your styles. Scenes need VesperTV 1.0.74 or newer; an older version leaves the style out.

      Made something nice? Share it on Discord or r/VesperTV. The format: vespertv.app/styles/make.
